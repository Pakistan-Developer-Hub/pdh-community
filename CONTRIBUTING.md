# 🤝 Contributing to Pakistan Developer Hub

Thank you for your interest in contributing to
Pakistan Developer Hub (PDH)!

We welcome students, beginners, developers, and open-source
enthusiasts who want to learn, collaborate, and grow together.

## 🌱 How You Can Contribute

- Share useful learning resources.
- Improve documentation.
- Report bugs and suggest improvements.
- Contribute to community projects.
- Help beginners and answer technical questions.
- Share ideas for improving PDH.

## 🚀 Contribution Process

1. Explore the repository.
2. Fork the repository.
3. Create a new branch.
4. Make your changes.
5. Write clear commit messages.
6. Push your changes.
7. Open a Pull Request.

## 📌 Contribution Guidelines

- Keep contributions relevant and meaningful.
- Write clear and understandable documentation.
- Be respectful and supportive.
- Avoid spam and unnecessary changes.
- Respect other contributors and their work.
- Give credit to original sources when required.

## 💚 Our Philosophy

We believe in learning by building, sharing knowledge,
and growing through collaboration.

Every meaningful contribution matters.
