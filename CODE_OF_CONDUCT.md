# 📜 Code of Conduct
## Pakistan Developer Hub (PDH)

### 🌍 Our Commitment

Pakistan Developer Hub (PDH) is committed to providing a
welcoming, inclusive, respectful, and collaborative environment
for everyone.

We believe that technology grows stronger when people from
different backgrounds, experiences, and skill levels work
together.

Whether you are a beginner, student, developer, freelancer,
mentor, or technology enthusiast, you are welcome here.

Our goal is to create a community where everyone can learn,
contribute, share ideas, and grow without fear of discrimination
or disrespect.

---

## 🤝 Our Standards

To maintain a positive and productive community, all members
and contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful, kind, and professional.
- Welcome people of all experience levels.
- Encourage beginners and support their learning journeys.
- Respect different opinions, perspectives, and backgrounds.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Communicate clearly and responsibly.
- Give credit to original creators and respect intellectual property.
- Collaborate openly and share knowledge.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on solutions rather than personal conflicts.

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable within PDH
communities, repositories, discussions, or activities:

- Harassment, bullying, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on identity or background.
- Hate speech, threats, or abusive language.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or intimidation.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Spam, scams, or misleading promotional content.
- Plagiarism or presenting someone else's work as your own.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

---

## 💬 Community Communication

Members are encouraged to:

- Keep discussions relevant and constructive.
- Ask questions respectfully.
- Help others whenever possible.
- Avoid unnecessary arguments and personal disputes.
- Respect moderators, maintainers, and community decisions.
- Use appropriate channels for different topics.

Disagreements are natural. However, they must always be
handled respectfully and professionally.

---

## 🛡️ Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior,
please report it to the Pakistan Developer Hub team.

**Official Contact Email:**

pakistandeveloperhub@gmail.com

When reporting an issue, provide relevant details and
available evidence where appropriate.

All reports will be reviewed carefully and handled
as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in
good faith is not acceptable.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

The PDH moderation and administration team is responsible
for reviewing reported incidents and taking appropriate action.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, actions may include:

1. A private warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restriction from community activities.
4. Temporary suspension.
5. Permanent removal from the community.

Serious violations may result in immediate action.

Enforcement decisions will aim to be fair, consistent,
and proportionate to the situation.

---

## 🌱 Our Shared Responsibility

Building a healthy developer community is everyone's
responsibility.

Every question, contribution, discussion, and collaboration
should help make PDH a better place to learn and grow.

We encourage every member to contribute positively and
help maintain the values of our community.

---

## 📌 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Pakistan Developer Hub
spaces and activities, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- WhatsApp community.
- Discord server.
- LinkedIn group.
- Facebook community.
- Official PDH events and collaborative activities.

---

## 📖 Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the principles of
the Contributor Covenant.

By participating in Pakistan Developer Hub, members
and contributors agree to follow these community standards.
